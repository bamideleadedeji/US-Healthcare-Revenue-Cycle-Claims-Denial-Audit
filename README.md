#  HealthRevenuePro USA | Claims Denial & Revenue Assurance Engine

HealthRevenuePro USA is an executive Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) audit suite engineered for US healthcare systems and RCM consultants. It automates claims denial analysis, categorizes ICD-10/CPT coding mismatches, and tracks payer leakage across Medicare, Medicaid, and commercial insurers to recover net revenue loss after appeal overheads.

---

##  Key Features
* **Payer Leakage Analytics:** Tracks denial exposure across Medicare, Medicaid, Blue Cross, UnitedHealth, Aetna, and Humana.
* **ICD-10/CPT Root-Cause Audit:** Categorizes claims by prior authorization gaps, coding errors, duplicate billing, and filing limits.
* **Net Recovery Modeling:** Calculates true net dollar recovery after factoring in per-claim appeal overhead costs.
* **Commercial Export Suite:** Generates downloadable CSV audit matrices and executive text reports for CFOs and RCM directors.

---

##  Tech Stack & Compliance
* **Language:** Python 3.10+
* **Framework:** Streamlit, Pandas, NumPy, Altair
* **Standards & Compliance:** Built using HIPAA Safe Harbor de-identification standards and CMS ICD-10/CPT billing conventions.
